Privacy & application policy

This page explains the current behavior of Wah-Lah KeyCounty. It is not a guarantee that a property, owner, or application has been independently verified.

Listings and owner authorization

A property is published only after the owner or authorized manager confirms permission to list it and share the listing photos. Listings and terms can change. Before arranging a viewing or sending money, independently confirm the identity and authority of the person offering the home, its current availability, the full costs, and the lease terms.

Applications

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or reserve a property. Application information is reviewed by the platform operator and may be shared with the relevant property owner or authorized manager for follow-up. Provide accurate information and submit only an application you intend to make.

Information collected

Depending on the form, you may provide your name, email, phone number, move-in date, monthly income, household size, pet details, employment and rental history, typed signature, and consent selections. Property owners may provide their contact details, property details, photos, and an authorization confirmation. Submitted information is stored to support application handling, listing review, and follow-up.

Do not enter a Social Security number, bank account number, card number, password, or other payment credentials into these forms. Those details are not needed for the current submission flow.

Fees and payments

Online payments are currently paused. Any fee amount shown is a configured reference only; this site does not collect it at application submission. Do not send money based only on a form submission or status page. Payment status, settlement, and payouts are not confirmed by a manually entered note.

Photos and owner submissions

People submitting a property must have authority to submit its details and permission to share its photos. Owner submissions are reviewed and do not publish automatically. Third-party candidate records remain private until the required verification is complete.

Questions or corrections

For questions about a submitted listing or application, contact Wah-Lah KeyCounty at admin@wah-lah.com or 334-538-3538. Avoid emailing highly sensitive personal information.
