Listing information

KeyCounty organizes housing information and links visitors to original listing sources. A listing summary may be out of date; check the original page for current price, availability, photos, terms, and contact instructions before making a decision.

Home Connection Service

Browsing listings is free. The one-time $35 Home Connection Service fee applies when you ask KeyCounty to follow up on a selected property, whether it is for sale, rent, or lease. The fee is separate from the property price, rent, deposits, and charges set by the property owner. Payment records your request for follow-up; it does not purchase or reserve the property or guarantee that the parties reach an agreement. A request is marked paid only after Paddle confirms payment.

Information collected

Forms may collect a name, email, phone number, housing preferences, property details, and messages needed to process a listing suggestion or application. Provide accurate information and only the personal information needed for the form.

Do not enter Social Security numbers, bank/card numbers, passwords, or one-time verification codes into KeyCounty forms. Online Home Connection Service checkout is handled on Paddle's secure hosted checkout page. KeyCounty does not receive or store raw card details. A payment is shown as paid only after provider confirmation. Coarse city estimates may be used to prefill the local listing filter; no precise device location is requested.

Site measurement

KeyCounty may record privacy-limited site events such as page visits, listing-grid views, filter use, connection requests, checkout starts, owner submissions, and campaign source labels in order to measure and improve the marketplace. Campaign labels, page paths, listing identifiers, event names, and timestamps may be stored; do not place names, email addresses, or other personal information into campaign URLs. KeyCounty does not use these events to request precise device location.

Corrections and removals

If a source listing is rented, sold, withdrawn, or has materially changed, send its original URL and the correction to admin@wah-lah.com. Listings can be hidden when their current status is stale or cannot be rechecked.
