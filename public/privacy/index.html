Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Wah-Lah KeyCounty (“KeyCounty,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit wah-lah-keycounty.hatchable.site, browse housing information, submit a housing request or property, or use a related service.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, email address, and phone number you submit.
  • Housing requests: preferred city, state, ZIP code or country; rent or purchase preference; budget; bedroom needs; timing; and messages.
  • Property submissions: owner or manager contact details, property address and description, asking price or rent, property features, source links, photo links, and your statement that you are authorized to submit the property and media.
  • Rental application information: selected property, contact details, desired move-in date, monthly income if supplied, number of occupants, pets, employment and rental-history information, typed signature, and consent selections. Submit only information requested by the form. Do not submit Social Security numbers, payment-card or bank details, passwords, or identity documents through ordinary site forms.
  • Payment and request records: request reference, fee amount, payment status, and payment-provider transaction identifiers. We do not receive or store full payment-card numbers through our hosted checkout integrations.
  • Technical and usage information: page paths, timestamps, listing identifiers, campaign parameters, event names, approximate referral domain, session identifier, and technical information made available by the hosting or security platform.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate and secure the site; display and review property information; process owner submissions; save housing, connection, and application requests; contact you about a request you submitted; send request confirmations and service messages; provide reference and payment-status information; reconcile payments; prevent spam, abuse, and duplicate submissions; maintain records; measure site performance; and comply with legal obligations.

3. Approximate location and site measurement

The site may request an approximate city or region from the third-party IP-location service ipapi.co to prefill an area filter or housing-request field. This is based on network information, may be inaccurate, and is not precise device GPS location. You can edit or clear the suggested area. The site also records limited first-party usage events, including page views, listing views, filters, request starts, and campaign source labels. Session identifiers may be stored in your browser session storage. Do not put names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal information in campaign URLs.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information only as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including:

  • Service providers: Hatchable provides application hosting and database infrastructure; email delivery services may deliver request confirmations and operational messages; Stripe provides hosted payment checkout and payment processing when enabled; and Paddle may process optional plan or subscription checkout where offered.
  • Property contacts: when you ask us to connect you about a selected property, we may share relevant contact and request details with the property owner, manager, or listing contact so the requested follow-up can occur. We do not promise that a third party will respond.
  • Listing and external websites: property links, embedded listing search, and third-party sites are controlled by their respective providers. When you follow an external link or use an embedded service, that provider may collect information under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect users or the public, investigate fraud or security incidents, or enforce our terms.
  • Business changes: information may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale of the relevant business, subject to applicable law.

5. Payments

The one-time Home Connection Service fee is currently displayed as $35. Browsing and general area requests are free. A connection or application request is not considered paid until the applicable payment provider confirms payment. When Stripe checkout is enabled for a one-time request, payment details are entered on Stripe-hosted checkout and handled under Stripe’s terms and privacy policy. Optional plans displayed on the pricing page may use Paddle-hosted checkout and are handled under Paddle’s terms and privacy policy. Payment providers may collect and process payment, billing, fraud-prevention, and transaction information directly. Do not send card or bank details to us by email or through site forms.

6. Data storage and security

Information submitted through the site is stored in the application database and related operational systems hosted through our service providers. We use access controls and provider security features intended to protect the information we handle. No website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use care when submitting information and contact us promptly if you believe your information has been misused.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, operate the service, maintain transaction and business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal or security requirements. Retention periods can vary by record type and provider. We do not promise a fixed deletion schedule where one has not been established.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may choose not to submit optional information. You can correct an area suggestion before submitting it. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you submitted, email admin@wah-lah.com and describe the request and the email address used for the submission. We may need to verify your request. We may retain information when required or permitted for legal, payment, fraud-prevention, security, or recordkeeping purposes. You can also control browser session storage and cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect site functions.

9. Children

The site is intended for adults seeking housing or submitting property information. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Third-party services and links

Our site may link to or embed external listing sources and payment-provider pages. We do not control those services, their content, or their privacy practices. Review the applicable provider’s privacy notice before using an external service.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates when this version was published. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact admin@wah-lah.com.
